Cooperative mission · team of 3

Only the leader answers — but the leader can’t see the evidence

A phishing message reached a recruiter’s inbox. The team leader holds the controls and is the only one who can mark the warning signs and decide — but sees none of the message. Two members each hold one half of the evidence and can only tell the leader what they see. The only way through is to talk.

Three people, three devices — each picks a different role. You can also deep-link roles: add ?role=leader, ?role=a or ?role=b to the URL.
