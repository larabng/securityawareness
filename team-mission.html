📋 How it works
  1. One device each. Everyone on the team opens this page on their own phone or laptop.
  2. Same team name. You all enter the same team name below — it’s just a label for your group.
  3. Take a seat → get a role. Each person takes a different seat: one Team leader and two members.
  4. Same email, different views. You all open the same email, but each role has different parts blacked out — and nobody is told who can see what.
  5. Talk together. Work out who can read which blacked-out parts and share them out loud, until the leader has the full picture.
  6. The leader decides, then reveals the answer together.
