02

OUR PRINCIPLE

“You learn the mechanism,
not the instructions.”

Learners step into the attacker’s perspective to see through an attack — then practise the defence. No real tools, templates or usable attack guides are ever taught.

HOW IT WORKS

A guided path from awareness to action

1 · Pre-survey

A baseline with a confidence rating per question — required before the modules open.

2 · Modules

Per weakness: expert video → hacker mission → reveal. Earns a key, a badge and points.

3 · Company gate

One key per module; together they open the gate — then the roles flip.

4 · Defense level

Now the staff: spot attacks in an inbox, build a streak, report them.

5 · Post-survey

The same situations, rethought — this measures what changed.

6 · Evaluation

Personal result, team leaderboard, and a CSV export for the facilitator.

GAMIFICATION

Recognition, turned into a game

🔑

Keys & company gate

One key per module; all keys together open the gate.

⭐

Points & difficulty

Harder weaknesses are worth more; hints cost points.

🎯

Themed badges

A badge per weakness mastered — a set to complete.

🔥

Defense streaks

A run of correct calls earns a bonus; a mistake resets it.

🏆

Team leaderboard

Played in pairs; only the top three teams are shown.

Why it works

Engaging, repeatable each season, and measurable.

07

THE DEFENSE LEVEL

Then the roles flip — now you guard the gate

An inbox mixes real attacks from the modules with harmless, genuine messages. Not every unusual message is an attack — the skill is telling them apart.

Process

Verify (2nd channel)

Report

Reject

The one habit:  Pause  →  check the context  →  verify through a known, independent channel  →  report.

08

MEASURING LEARNING

We measure the change, not just completion

Before & after

Parallel surveys of equal difficulty, with no visible answers — a clean before/after comparison.

Confidence too

Each answer carries a self-rating, so we see not only what people know, but how sure they are.

CSV export

The facilitator exports all responses anonymised — ready to analyse across a group.

WHAT IT’S BASED ON

Grounded in real cases and how people learn

Real cases

  • ManpowerGroup franchise, Lansing 2025 — ~145,000 people affected
  • NCSC Switzerland: 970 CEO-fraud reports in 2025 (484 in 2023)

Learning science

  • Perspective-taking makes the lesson stick
  • Active recall over passive watching
  • Before/after measurement of the effect

Safety guardrail

  • Recognition over instruction
  • No usable attack tooling or templates
  • Fictional company and data throughout

Figures: public reports & NCSC Switzerland — verify before external use.
